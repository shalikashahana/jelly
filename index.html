The Anatomy of a Squish

As you scroll through the page, vertical velocity transfers momentum directly into the jelly. Watch how the soft body transitions through four states of viscoelastic mechanics.

State 1: Impact
01

Impact & Deformation

When external kinetic energy makes contact, the jelly yields instantly. The impacted points deflect inward while volume preservation forces neighboring regions to bulge outwards along normal vectors.

Deformation profile Radial Flattening + Lateral Expansion
02

Elastic Tension & Rebound

Hooke's restoring springs accumulate potential energy proportionally to displacement. At peak deflection, velocity hits zero and reverses sharply, snapping the body upward with elastic overshoot.

Potential energy E_p = 1/2 · k · Δx²
03

Viscous Harmonic Jiggle

The body does not simply stop at rest. Instead, underdamped harmonic resonance produces multiple secondary ripples, traveling across the perimeter vertices like ripples on a pond.

Damping ratio Underdamped (ζ < 1) Resonance
04

Thermal Equilibrium & Idle Breath

Frictional damping smoothly dissipates excess energy into stillness. Yet even at equilibrium, low-frequency micro-pulsations keep the gelatin feeling alive, warm, and ready for your next interaction.

Equilibrium 0.4 Hz Ambient Breathing Cycle
